# Geoffrey Hinton example

Editable bilingual example, added once before existing projects. Navy / Source Serif 4 + Source Sans 3; Korean Noto Serif KR + Pretendard. Public exports and social metadata identify this as an unofficial Folio example.

## Sources (checked 2026-09-16)

- [Official website](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~hinton/): biography, research, public contact email and current emeritus role.
- [Official CV](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~hinton/shortcv.pdf), dated 6 January 2025: education and selected appointments. The PhD was awarded in **1978**, although study dates are 1972–1975. The CV section links to this original PDF; it is not a fabricated or translated CV. Normal PDF upload remains available.
- [Nobel Prize](https://www.nobelprize.org/laureate/1038): 2024 Physics award, jointly with John J. Hopfield.
- [ACM SIGAI](https://sigai.acm.org/main/2019/04/01/472/): 2018 Turing Award, presented in 2019, jointly with Yoshua Bengio and Yann LeCun.
- [Deep learning](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature14539), [Dropout](https://jmlr.org/papers/v15/srivastava14a.html), [t-SNE](https://jmlr.org/papers/v9/vandermaaten08a.html): publication metadata; titles stay in their original English in both languages.
- [Nobel Prize's lecture video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XDE9DjpcSdI): Boltzmann Machines, 8 December 2024.

Biographical and research summaries are original paraphrases; Korean is a Folio translation, not an official translation. This is a selected example, not an exhaustive CV.

## Storage

IndexedDB initializes projects and `migration:geoffrey-hinton-v1` atomically. Existing edits, attachments and deleted projects remain intact; concurrent tabs re-read current saved data. Deleted examples are not re-added. A failed portrait download leaves existing projects usable and retries on the next visit. Empty legacy workspaces stay empty except for this one new example.
